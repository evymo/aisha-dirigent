### Description  *(max 4000)*
```
AISHA Dirigent is your personal companion for taking part in health and research programmes with confidence. It brings your study journey, daily health tracking, questionnaires, and secure communication together in one private, GDPR-first app.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Follow your story — see every step of your care or research journey on a clear board and timeline that updates automatically as things happen.

• Track how you feel — log quick daily check-ins for mood, energy, sleep, pain and medication, and watch your trends over time.

• Take part in studies — discover programmes that match your interests, review exactly what each one involves, give informed consent, and enrol in a tap.

• Answer questionnaires — complete scheduled questionnaires with clear, localized questions, and pick up right where you left off.

• Ask AISHA — chat with an AI assistant that understands the context of your own story, and message your care team directly and securely.

• Stay in control of your data — review and manage every consent, see exactly who you share data with, and check your compliance summary at any time.

• Your wallet & services — see your subscriptions and the services available to you in one place.

• Stay motivated — gentle rewards and a friendly leaderboard help you keep up your routine.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your data is protected end to end. Sign in securely with your organization account and unlock the app with Face ID. You decide what you share, and you can review or withdraw any consent at any time. We never sell your data, and we never use it for advertising.

AISHA Dirigent is made for members of partner health and research programmes. Some features require an invitation from your programme.

This app does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow the guidance of your healthcare professional.
```
