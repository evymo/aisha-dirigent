Ask an electrician

Straight answers to the questions I get asked most on the job — no jargon, no upselling.

Why do my lights flicker?

Usually a loose connection somewhere on the circuit — annoying, but worth checking promptly, because the same loose joint that flickers a light can also heat up. If it's more than one room, it's worth a proper look.

Do I really need to upgrade my fuse board?

Not always. If yours still uses rewireable fuses or has no RCD protection, an upgrade is a genuine safety improvement. If it's a modern unit in good order, I'll tell you to keep your money.

Is my old wiring safe?

Old isn't automatically unsafe — but cloth or rubber-sheathed cable, or anything that looks scorched, is worth testing. A quick inspection tells you where you actually stand instead of guessing.
