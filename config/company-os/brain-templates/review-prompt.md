# review-prompt.md — the "anyone test" checklist

> Business brain file (user-owned). The review agent runs this checklist on
> everything before it ships. Nothing publishes without passing it.

## Anyone test

- [ ] Would someone with zero context understand this in one read?
- [ ] Is every factual claim backed by the brain (what-i-do, wins-log) or a source?
- [ ] Is it in my voice per style-rules.md (no banned phrases)?
- [ ] Does it have exactly one clear next action for the reader?

## Accuracy

- [ ] Numbers, dates, names verified against brain files
- [ ] No invented offers, prices, or availability
- [ ] No confidential or secret material (keys, client names under NDA)

## Ship quality

- [ ] Right format for the channel
- [ ] Nothing that would embarrass me in 6 months

## Verdict format

Return: `SHIP` or `FIX FIRST` + numbered issues, each with the exact spot and
why it fails the checklist. The human makes the final call.
