Welcome — your first week

Updated 2 days ago · 4 min read

Welcome aboard. This page is the short version of everything you need in week one — skim it now, and come back when a question comes up. Nothing here is secret; if something's unclear, that's a bug in the handbook, so please fix it.

Your first day

Someone from your team will meet you and walk you through accounts, the calendar, and where to find things. Don't try to read everything at once — the handbook is here so you don't have to remember it. Your only real job this week is to ask a lot of questions and write down anything that surprised you.

Where to ask

Default to asking in the open, in a shared channel, rather than a private message — that way the answer helps the next person too, and often becomes a new handbook page. There are no silly questions in your first month; the people who ask the most tend to ramp up the fastest.

Tip: Found something out of date? Click “Suggest an edit” on any page — small fixes are the most welcome contribution there is.
